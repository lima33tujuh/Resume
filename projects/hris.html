Human Resources Information System

I hope this application helps employees access their work-related administrative needs through a single digital platform, providing a simple and structured experiencefrom clocking in and out and viewing leave data to accessing payslips.

Role UI/UX Designer
Focus UX Research & UI Design
Tools Figma

Simplifying everyday employee management

In this project, I focused on UX research and analysis to understand user needs regarding access to information and employee administrative tasks. The design process included developing user flows, wireframes, UI design, and prototyping.

Creating consistency the HRIS experience

A design system is used to maintain consistency in visuals and interaction patterns across the HRIS interface. Typography, color, spacing, and reusable components are organized to create a structured and user-friendly experience.

Design system - colors, typography, spacing and visual elements.

Building a scalable interface

Interface components are designed to be reusable across different parts of the system. This approach helps maintain consistency while making the interface easier to develop and maintain.

Component library - reusable UI elements for the EMR interface.

Structuring everyday employee tasks

The user flow is designed to make key employee activities easier to perform and understand. This flow encompasses processes such as attendance tracking, submitting and reviewing leave requests, and accessing payslip information.
