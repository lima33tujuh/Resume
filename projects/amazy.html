Amazy Family Resto is a restaurant brand under PT MagFood Amazy Internasional, part of PT MagFood Inovasi Pangan, established in February 2001.

PT MagFood operates in the food seasoning and product development sector, serving food industry needs across Indonesia from large to small-scale snack companies. For Amazy, I designed a playful brand identity that appeals to families: the logo, menu design, and supporting visual assets.
